# Art Direction — "The Emperor's Dinner Parties" (Elagabalus Short)

**Design read:** a museum at night. Real Roman marble, mosaics and 19th-century
history paintings, lit like candlelight — warm, low, oily — with the camera acting
like a curious, slightly cruel guest moving through the room.

**Palette:** candle amber highlights, umber shadows, crushed blacks. One accent:
**Tyrian crimson (#B3122E)** — the imperial purple-red — reserved for the words that
carry the horror (LIONS, FRIGHT, WAX, GLASS, BURIED ALIVE, KILLED).

**Type:** Cinzel 900 (Roman inscriptional capitals), all caps, 2–3 words per page,
word-by-word pop, sitting at ~60% height — above the Shorts UI.

**Motion character:** hard cuts only, no dissolves. Every plate moves: fast push-in
on the hook, slow creeping pans in the body, a snap zoom onto the lion's teeth on
"fright". Impacts get a 2-frame exposure flash.

**Signature device:** the loop. The film opens and closes on the same marble face at
the same framing, so "…with this Roman emperor—" lands on frame one:
"This Roman emperor let lions loose…".

**Texture:** animated film grain + heavy vignette + warm grade, so plates from
different museums read as one film.

**Anti-patterns:** no AI-generated people, no stock "ancient Rome" CGI, no
templated title cards, no dissolves, no lower-third boxes.
